About STAY Properties

Independent, local, and personal since 2019.

Our Story

STAY Properties was founded in 2019 by a small team of agents who left larger franchises to build something more personal. We believe buying a home shouldn't feel like a transaction — it should feel like someone is actually on your side.

Our mission is to make property buying transparent, personal, and stress-free by pairing every client with an agent who genuinely understands their needs. Our vision is to become Gauteng's most trusted independent estate agency.
